# Customer & Product Analytics — SQL Reporting Views

A comprehensive SQL project focused on extracting actionable insights from customer and product sales data. This project simulates enterprise-grade reporting layers typically used by analytics teams in large organizations to support strategic decision-making.

## 📊 Project Highlights

### 🧍 Customer Analytics View
- Segments customers by age group and purchasing behavior (VIP, Regular, New).
- Key metrics include:
  - Total orders, sales, quantity, product diversity
  - Recency of last order
  - Average order value (AOV)
  - Monthly spend and customer lifespan

### 📦 Product Performance View
- Categorizes products into High-, Mid-, and Low-Performers based on sales revenue.
- Key metrics include:
  - Sales and quantity sold
  - Unique customers per product
  - Average selling price and order revenue
  - Monthly revenue and product lifespan
